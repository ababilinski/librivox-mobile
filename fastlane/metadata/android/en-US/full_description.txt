Listen to classic audiobooks on your phone, or cast them to a compatible speaker or TV. Download books before you head out and keep listening offline.

Start with LibriVox recordings, read by volunteers. You can also enable Lit2Go, Project Gutenberg through Gutendex, and Wolne Lektury in Settings. Wolne Lektury includes Polish audiobooks.

Find books by title or author. LibriVox search also supports chapters and readers. Save books to your library, keep your listening progress, and add bookmarks with notes.

Adjust playback speed, set a sleep timer, and move between chapters. Background playback and Android media controls let you keep listening while using other apps.

Casting works with compatible Google Cast speakers, TVs, and receivers on the same local network as your phone. You can choose an output from the player.

No account is required. The app has no ads, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Your library, progress, bookmarks, settings, and downloads are stored on your device.
