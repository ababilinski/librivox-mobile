01 / Discovery

A library of possibilities.

Start with featured books or go looking for an old favorite. Choose the collections and languages that interest you.

  • Search for a book or author. LibriVox search also supports chapters and readers.
  • Choose your collections. Discover LibriVox, Lit2Go, and Wolne Lektury. Meet the collections.
  • Look before you listen. Book details bring together descriptions, chapters, and original source links.

02 / Listening

Stay with the story.

The cover, the chapter, and your place in it. Listening controls stay close, so a small adjustment doesn’t interrupt a good book.

  • Find your pace. Change playback speed and skip forward or back.
  • Settle in for the night. A sleep timer stops playback after your chosen interval.
  • Carry on in the background. Use Android media controls while you move between apps.

03 / Your library

Pick up the thread.

Some books take an afternoon. Others stay with you for weeks. Your library makes it easy to return.

  • Save a book for later. Keep a personal collection of the stories you want to hear.
  • Remember a moment. Add bookmarks with notes and come back to a specific passage.
  • Keep your progress. Your library and listening data are stored on your device, without an account.

04 / Wherever you listen

Go where the day takes you.

Away from a connection or at home with a speaker. Bring the next chapter along.

  • Download for the journey. Save chapters while connected and play downloaded audio offline.
  • Choose a bigger sound. Cast to compatible Google Cast speakers and TVs on your local network.
