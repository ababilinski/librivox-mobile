# Phone usage scenes

The full built-in Imagegen prompt set is saved in `prompts.json`. The first step creates a phone with a blank display in a setting that fits the feature: a desk with a Google Home speaker, a phone held at a cafe table, or a bedside nightstand.

The second step places an alternate real Android capture inside the display. Imagegen insertion previews were inspected, then the final SVG screen layers were rendered from the original screenshots to keep the text, icons, artwork, and playback states unchanged. The screen layers are fitted proportionally and masked at the physical display corners and camera cutout.

Captions are composed outside the screen. The photographs illustrate app usage; they are not photographs of the device tests. The three final phone images are labeled as AI assets in Play Console. Capture descriptions and source hashes are recorded in `provenance.json`.
